Five Materials Holding One Room.

The room runs on five materials, each chosen for how it ages. Limestone, pine, leather, tweed, brass. Nothing chasing trend, nothing afraid of wear.

Color goes in through what the materials already are, not through accents or paint. Limestone takes light without bouncing it. Pine warms underfoot. Cognac leather darkens with use.
