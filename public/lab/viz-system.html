The chart system
Ink on paper

Four shapes, one constitution. Every chart on the site is one of these, drawn from the same stroke scale. Specimens below use real shipped numbers only.

Fine line · 2px full inkTracks, rings, baselines, rules. Recedes by being thin, never by being grey. Nothing wispy.
Heavy mark · 9px, round capsThe datum. Four to five times the fine weight. Solid ink, no outlines, no fills-with-borders.
Grey · 45%, data onlySecond phases, remainders, captions. Structure never fades — grey is a data state, not a background voice.
One emphasis · the pillA single label may sit reversed in a black pill. That is the whole emphasis kit. No accent colour anywhere.
The density budgetNothing on this sheet labels more than ~10 things, and nothing sets type on a curve or a slant. A chart that wants more must aggregate, transpose until its labels sit level, or move the roll-call into a caption. Rotated label fields and per-item annotation are how the old charts crept back in.

Rings — the circle form

01 · REPLACES EVERY WHEEL, DONUT, PIE, BUBBLE

Concentric fine tracks, each running the full circle. The value is the same circle drawn heavy over an arc — share of the whole, start fixed at 12 o'clock, clockwise. Hub stays empty or holds one stat. A second phase on the same ring is grey; two greys maximum, never a second hue. Read outside-in, largest first.

Source: Ivy Park campaign, 8 channels · 116 assets (shipped in campaign-blast-radius).

Sticks — the default bar

02 · TWO POSTURES, ONE MARK

Bars are heavy strokes, not rectangles — round caps, air between them, one fine baseline, no grid. Grounded rises from the baseline; floating spans min to max when the value is honestly a range. The one emphasis: a single label in the black pill.

Sources: the same eight channel counts (grounded) · the homepage type tokens, px at narrowest to widest viewport (floating).

Stripes — the editorial bar

03 · ONE PER STUDY, AT MOST

When a bar must carry mass rather than mark a position: the value is the same heavy mark as the sticks, lying down — a round-capped bar riding just above a panel of fine stripes. One mark, two postures; only the mass is textured. The poster voice — used once per study, for the hero magnitude. The shipped stepped mass (blast radius) is this family's solid-ink sibling and stays.

Source: the four largest of the same eight channels — a stated subset, not a re-count.

Dots — the matrix

04 · PRESENCE ACROSS TWO AXES

Rows by columns, dots in exactly two sizes — the small dot is a slot, the large disc is a hit. Both are solid ink: small means small, never faint. No shading between the two sizes; density is how many discs, not how dark. For membership, coverage, anything true/false with texture.

Source: the Ivy Park ledger's type specimen — six campaign words stepping through Avenir Next's four upright cuts, as shipped.

Motion

TWO TIERS · THE SECOND ONE IS RATIONED

Every chart on this sheet is animating. Arrival runs once, in the direction the chart's own logic runs — arcs sweep from twelve o'clock, sticks grow off the baseline, a stripe mass fills and its cap lands last, dots put the empty grid down before the hits. Then it stops. The reading head is the part that repeats: a slow cursor stepping one item at a time for as long as the chart is on screen, so a reader who stays is walked through the series.

It moves labels, never marksLifting a bar to say "look here" would misstate the number. The label goes grey → ink; everything drawn holds perfectly still.
Only where there is a seriesA single-datum chart has nothing to walk and gets arrival alone. Rationing is what keeps a repeat from becoming a fidget.
The pill is immuneIt is an authored claim about which datum matters. A passing cursor does not get to borrow that emphasis.
Off screen it stopsThe clock releases when the chart leaves, holds under reduced motion, and accumulates capped deltas so a backgrounded tab never fast-forwards.

Retired

ON ADOPTION

Ghosted hairlines as structure (the kit's 14%/8% tokens) · opacity as hierarchy · the press-red accent · pies, donuts, wheels, bubble fields · square caps · rectangles-with-fills as bars · legends. Colour survives only where hue is the datum — palette charts keep real swatches on ink bones. Emphasis is the pill, hierarchy is size, and everything drawn is solid.
