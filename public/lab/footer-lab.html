A · The Indexfx-index-grid
Numbered archive grid. Hover a thumbnail: the number swaps for the title. Sparse on purpose. A black 96-cell index, 8 columns collapsing to 4 at 760px, with 12 thumbnails scattered across digital, creative direction, interiors, type, and motion, plus three news fragments, a contact cell, and the wordmark at cell 36. Thumbs sit grayscale and take color on hover while the cell number swaps to the project title in mono; every 8 seconds one thumbnail quietly relocates to an empty cell (paused off-screen, skipped entirely under reduced motion). Knobs: swap interval (8000ms in the IIFE), cell height clamps in .ig-cell, and the filled-cell positions are just DOM order so re-scattering is a copy-paste.

B · The Ledgerfx-ledger
Info table up top, then one strip per discipline with a rotated vertical label. Strips scroll sideways. A one-page CV table closes the study: a four-column mono info ledger (contact, services, about, last update with the three news lines) over three discipline rows, each with a rotated vertical-rl mono label and a hidden-scrollbar thumbnail strip (8 digital, 8 creative, 7 interiors) that drag-scrolls with the mouse and swipes natively on touch. Thumbs sit grayscale and bloom to color on hover, with var(--r) as the only curve on the page. Knobs: card width (168px desktop, 132px mobile), the 0.14 hairline alpha, and the grayscale hover can be dropped for always-color strips.

C · The Tickersfx-tickers
Stacked marquees, alternating direction and fill. Hover pauses a row; a thumbnail chip follows the cursor. Four ink-ground marquee rows carry all 28 case studies at clamp(48px, 9vw, 130px), alternating direction and filled versus outlined type, each drifting at its own speed (84s to 110s) so the rows fall out of phase. Hovering a row pauses it and dims everything but the hovered title while a 160px thumbnail chip with radius var(--r) trails the cursor; below sit a thin mono news marquee and a flat contact row. Knobs: per-row --t-dur custom properties for speed, the separator glyph in .t-sep, and chip size in .t-chip plus the matching w/h constants in moveChip.

D · The Corridorfx-corridor
Walls of drifting image slices around a quiet contact form. Hover a slice to open it. A black corridor: two walls of 20 shredded thumbnail slivers each (all 28 projects appear across the pair), drifting vertically on 40 to 80s loops, desaturated until hover widens a slice to 120px, restores color, and reveals a rotated mono title linking to the study. The center stays calm: an underlined white contact form with a mailto fallback (also the no-JS form action), the three contact links, the case-study count, and the wordmark. Knobs: wall width clamp(240px, 30vw, 480px), drift amplitude in the fx-corridor-drift keyframes (14px), hover width in the .c-slice hover rule, and the L and R index arrays that set slice order per wall.

E · Spine Indexfx-wildcard
Free brief: vertical type against horizontal as the core move. A black bookshelf ending to the white page: six case studies stand as vertical spines in six equal hairline columns, bottoms resting on a single white shelf rule, thumbnails tucked at the base like colophons, with the wordmark running bottom-to-top down the left rail against the horizontal mono system (label, contact, news with CSS-added period dots). Spines rise onto the shelf with a staggered reveal on scroll, and thumbnails hold grayscale until hover restores the photography's color, the only color in the composition. Knobs: per-spine title sizes live in the .fxw-s1 through .fxw-s6 rules (desktop clamps plus fixed mobile sizes), and the shelf reveal is gated behind the fxw-anim class so it degrades to static without JS or with reduced motion.

G · The Shelffx-shelf
E as a horizontal rail of all 28. Titles sit down on their thumbnails and are bottom-anchored, so the staggered tops come free from name length rather than from hand-set offsets. Hover drops a paper curtain: the type inverts per-pixel at the curtain's edge because it is difference-blended, so running the shelf fast cascades. The title lifts a few pixels, no spin.
