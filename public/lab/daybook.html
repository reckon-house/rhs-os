Framed, not ported

Four Sally portal demos went into the case study today: Jim chat, PDP audit, email build, Figma build. The handoff note proposed one React component per demo. The port went the other way, and the demos run as documents, framed.

Three reasons. The chrome is extracted verbatim from the portal, and re-deriving 3,200 lines forks it from the thing it copies. The selectors are names like .header and .message, which collide the moment they are inlined. And the portal is a fixed 1120px internal tool, so a component port faces the same scaling problem and gives up the isolation for nothing.
