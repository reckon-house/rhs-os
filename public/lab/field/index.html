The field · lab

One field. Filter, chat, history, prediction, navigation. Nine different ways it could be the same thing.

Each page below is a separate answer, built on the house's own studies and the same small brain, so what differs between them is only how the field works. None is finished. Each is meant to be tried for a minute and judged on one question: where do I type, and what does typing do?

The board's current answer is a sheet that opens under the bar. These are the roads not taken, and two or three of them are better.

01 The SentenceThe prose is the input and the output The statement itself is the form. Every underlined term is a live filter, typing replaces the sentence's variable, and the house answers by rewriting the sentence. History is the old sentences, faded, stacked beneath. 02 The LedgerOne editable line of everything you did Filter, question and study all append tokens to a single line: interiors · kitchens · Hill Country home. Delete a token to go back. The next token, in grey, is the house's guess. The line is the URL. 03 The SlashThe rail's chips already say "/ Info" Type a slash and the rail's options appear inline as a palette. /interiors filters, /next walks, a plain sentence asks. The history is the command log and the house pre-fills the next command as a ghost. 04 The ColumnsEach question opens a column to the right The board already pages sideways. Here the conversation is the horizontal axis: ask, and a new column opens with the answer; page left to go back. The field sits at the head of the newest column. 04b The Columns, second cutThe homepage stays; columns push it right The owner's spec after living with 04. The house is the last column and every question, shelf or study opens a column before it, no cap, no back. Columns scroll on their own; the page only moves sideways. Every head is a field: type to narrow that column, Enter to open the next. × folds a column away. The rail holds the path. 05 The MarginThe rail is the conversation The chips are past lines, the field is the newest chip, and the house's prediction is a ghost chip at the bottom. Navigation and chat become the same objects, in the column that already holds the navigation. 06 The TypewriterThe line never moves; the paper does The field is a fixed line low on the page, like a terminal's. Enter commits the line and the paper scrolls up. Oldest at the top, newest just above the cursor. The opposite ordering to the board's, to feel the difference. 07 The ReadoutThe field is a caption of where you are No input at rest. The line at the top says what you're looking at, Interiors · 4 of 12, and what the house would show next. Click the line and it becomes the input. The field is the site's own status line. 08 Two SpeedsKeystrokes filter, sentences ask While you type, the field is a filter and the work dims live. Press Enter and it is a question the house answers. The history marks which lines were which. One field, two verbs, and the difference is the return key. 09 The GhostThe prediction lives inside the field A shell's autosuggestion, borrowed whole: the house's guess is grey text ahead of the caret, in the same line. → takes it, ↑ recalls, Enter on an empty field goes where the house would.
