# sally-push

The editorial pass on his Sally Beauty concepts (2 Oct 2026, two rounds):
Satoshi in place of Founders Grotesk, white paper in place of the
Natural-scale panels, no rules (rounded cards and white space separate
things, after rhode and OUAI, mixed with lululemon), stories told in
order. Hand-made, unlike `../sally-system/`, which the import script
rewrites.

- `index.html`: before and after, live, side by side, desktop and phone
- `colorfest.html`: Colorfest (concept 4), with the only-at-Sally brands as the festival lineup
- `pride.html`: the Pride takeover (concept 6), the looks on cards in their own shades, the $250,000 pledge as the one number
- `the-color-issue.html`: The Edit (concept 10) as a magazine issue
- `the-gloss.html`, `the-sale.html`: the editorial and sale emails
- `push.css`: tokens, type, both layouts; the vivid shades (violet, teal, cherry, blue) are sampled from his photographs

Pictures come from `../sally-system/assets/`. Satoshi is the site's own
`/fonts/Satoshi-Variable.woff2`.
