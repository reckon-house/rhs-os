The cover story

Color is a love language.

This summer, the boldest thing you can do is look like yourself.

Three colorists on the shades they keep mixing, the rules worth breaking, and why your roots are nobody's business but yours.

01On going bolder

Nobody ever regretted the brave color.

There is a moment, somewhere between the second and third bowl of dye, when you decide who you are this season. Maybe it is copper. Maybe it is a violet so loud it argues with the sun. Either way, the choice is yours, and it is a good one.

We asked three colorists what they reach for when a client says "surprise me." Their answers had nothing to do with trends. They talked about light, about skin, about the small thrill of a shade that looks like a decision.

Your hair is the one thing you get to rewrite whenever you want.

Mara V. · Colorist, 14 years behind the chair
