01The how-to

Copper, by hand.

The trick the pros will tell you for free: patience does most of the work. The rest is just paying attention.

  1. Start on clean, dry hair.
  2. Section it in four, like you mean it.
  3. Mids and ends first, roots last.
  4. Rinse cool, then seal it with a gloss.
