Section 03: Brand System

Built Without a Brand Guide

Beyoncé's team brought the photography and the name. No brand guide, no palette, no type system.

Six weeks meant building the visual language while the page got built. The polygon, the type at volume, the run from black-and-white into color. One system carrying a launch that lived on a single URL.

I

The System

The polygon started as a crop mask for portraits. Angled edges against the rectangular photo grid gave the athlete imagery a geometric tension, sport against fashion. It rotated on scroll, scaled from thumbnail to full-bleed, and carried from the hero banner through product carousels into email headers.

Type ran large enough to compete with the photography, not caption it. Short declarative lines, present tense. The whole experience moved from black-and-white into color as it scrolled.

The Polygon · Signature device
