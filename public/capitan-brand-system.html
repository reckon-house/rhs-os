Section 04: Marks & Materials

Identity Built for the Material

Marks made for stamp, stitch, embroidery, print. Tested against the process before they earned a place.

Leather takes pressure. Denim takes thread. Every color, face, and lockup had to survive the surface it lands on and still read at thumbnail. Range before decoration.

Constructed on a grid

The System

Primary logo, secondary badges, typographic lockups, illustrative assets. Each one keeps its shape under load, because range comes before decoration.

Northwest handles scale. Oldman brings character. The bull skull lockup constructs on a geometric grid, and the buffalo lives as a stamp first, an illustration second. Every choice traces back to where the boot ends up.

Northwest · Display & signage
Bold geometric sans. Carries scale on stamps, banners, and labels. Holds at any size, survives any process.
Oldman · Wordmark & headlines
Hand-cut serif with rough edges. Carries character on packaging and apparel. The primary wordmark.
