**Status:** Stub. Add a canonical Chimero essay here.

**Recommended pieces to capture:**
- "The Web's Grain" (2015) — on designing with the medium rather than against it
- "Everything Easy is Hard Again" (2018) — on cycles in design and tooling
- "What Screens Want" (2013) — on designing for time-based media
- "Designing in the Borderlands" (2015)

**Use for:** essayistic prose by a designer who writes well. The "art register" version of intellectual seriousness. Where Anthropic's voice is researcher-formal, Chimero's is designer-personal — confident point of view, careful phrasing, willingness to wander into a metaphor before landing the claim.

**Specific moves to study:**
- Long paragraphs that develop an idea rather than itemize it
- Metaphor as load-bearing structure (the web's grain, borderlands, etc.) — not decoration but actual scaffolding for the argument
- Confidence about taste-level claims without resorting to "obviously" or "of course"
- Personal anecdotes used to ground theoretical points, never as throat-clearing
- Section breaks used to reset rhythm, not to subdivide content
- Endings that land somewhere real instead of restating the thesis
- Comfortable being long when the argument needs the room

**When to reach for this:** before drafting the homepage manifesto, principles section, About page copy, or any House Staples piece that's making an argument about design or craft. Pair with Simmons for cadence; pair with Anthropic for restraint. Chimero sits between the two registers.

**Anti-pattern to watch for:** Chimero's prose can occasionally tip into precious — careful in a way that calls attention to its own care. The goal is the underlying rigor, not the surface poetry.
